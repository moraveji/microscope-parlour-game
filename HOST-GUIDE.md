# Host guide — official imported challenges

Use this answer key at the adult station. Prepare your own safe, marked pickup stations; this guide does not identify plants. Start at 4x and follow each mission’s preparation instructions.

## Team 1 — Pomni’s Lost Memories

### 1. 🪻 Purple Petal Portal

**Pickup:** Adult-prepared, marked specimen station; approve a safe sample before play.

**Clue:** Pomni has lost her purple world! Find a fallen piece of a tiny purple flower.

**Preparation:** Adult preparation: tiny flat petal on dry slide, 4x.

**Question:** What is a petal part of?

- A. Root
- B. Flower
- C. Rock

**Correct answer:** B — Flower

**Hint:** This colorful structure attracts pollinators.

**Explanation:** Petals are parts of flowers.

### 2. 🧩 Zooble's Broken Pieces

**Pickup:** Adult-prepared, marked specimen station; approve a safe sample before play.

**Clue:** Zooble broke into tiny jagged pieces! Find a little rock with sharp-looking corners.

**Preparation:** Adult preparation: one tiny grain on dry slide, 4x only.

**Question:** Most ordinary rocks contain what?

- A. Minerals
- B. Leaves
- C. Feathers

**Correct answer:** A — Minerals

**Hint:** Natural rocks are made from geological ingredients.

**Explanation:** Rocks typically contain one or more minerals.

### 3. 🍃 Miniature Network

**Pickup:** Adult-prepared, marked specimen station; approve a safe sample before play.

**Clue:** Find a tiny green leaf full of secret pathways.

**Preparation:** Adult preparation: thin flat leaf on dry slide, 4x.

**Question:** What do leaf veins move?

- A. Water and sugars
- B. Insects
- C. Pebbles

**Correct answer:** A — Water and sugars

**Hint:** They're the leaf's delivery system.

**Explanation:** Veins contain plant transport tissues.

### 4. 🧅 Onion Dimension

**Pickup:** Adult-prepared, marked specimen station; approve a safe sample before play.

**Clue:** Find a vegetable with layers like a secret dimension.

**Preparation:** An adult peels a tiny transparent onion membrane and lays it flat on a clean glass slide. Start dry at 4x. If an adult chooses the optional single water drop, use a suitable coverslip and keep it away from the lens. Seeing individual cells is not required; use the science hint if the image is unclear.

**Question:** What are the box-like compartments sometimes visible in onion skin?

- A. Plant cells
- B. Sand grains
- C. Insect eggs

**Correct answer:** A — Plant cells

**Hint:** They are basic building blocks of living plants.

**Explanation:** Onion epidermis often reveals rectangular plant cells, but clarity varies.
